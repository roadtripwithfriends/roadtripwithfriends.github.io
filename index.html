RoadTrip With Friends

Drive. Dodge. Laugh.

Cannons along the road fire faces at your car. Add your friends' faces and see who you can dodge.

Learn more about the game

The game

  • Swipe or tap to switch lanes and dodge the flying faces
  • Collect fuel cans – run out, and everyone has to get out and push!
  • Add photos of your friends and put them on the road
  • Daily road rules, new cars to unlock and a Game Center world leaderboard
